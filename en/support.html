Support

If something is not working or you have a suggestion, please open a GitHub issue. Do not post server addresses, keys or passwords.

Frequently asked

I cannot connect

Press and hold the server in the host list and choose Connection diagnosis. It shows whether DNS, the port, the host key or authentication is failing.

It says the host key changed

Do not connect unless you reinstalled or replaced the server. If you are sure, the diagnosis screen lets you forget the saved host key.

AI is not working

On-device AI works on devices that support Apple Intelligence with it turned on. Risky-command warnings and rule-based help keep working either way.

Subscription and lifetime purchase

Buy or restore Pro in Settings > Hushterm Pro. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
