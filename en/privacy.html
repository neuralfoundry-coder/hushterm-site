Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Hushterm ("the app") is an SSH client for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the app does with information.

In short

The developer does not collect your data. There is no account, no developer server, and no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

What is stored on your device only

  • Your host list (name, address, port, user name, tag, notes), command history, snippets, port-forwarding rules and saved host keys (known_hosts) live in the app's local storage only.
  • Passwords and SSH private keys are stored in this device's Keychain (this-device-only) and are not synced to iCloud Keychain. Secure Enclave keys cannot be exported.
  • Deleting the app deletes this information.

Network use

  • The app connects only to the SSH servers you specify. Terminal input and output go only between you and that server.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit; the developer does not receive payment information.

On-device AI

  • Command suggestions, error analysis, output summaries and connection-diagnosis explanations run on this device with Apple's on-device model (Foundation Models). Commands, output and host details are not sent off the device.
  • The risky-command warning is decided by rules inside the app, not by AI.
  • Voice input uses on-device speech recognition only, and recordings are not stored.

Permissions

  • Face ID: app lock and confirming Secure Enclave key use.
  • Microphone & speech recognition: only when you speak a command.
  • Local network: needed to reach servers on your network.
  • Notifications: to tell you an agent is waiting for approval (optional).

Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. A list of open-source components is in Settings > Open-source licenses. Crash reports provided by Apple may reach the developer only if you allow it in your device settings, and they do not identify you.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Contact us through GitHub issues. Please do not post server addresses, keys or passwords in an issue.
